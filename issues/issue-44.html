ISSUE #44 • 2026-W45 Monday, November 2, 2026 (Oct 26 – Nov 2, 2026)
THREAT LEVEL: Moderate

WordPress Booking Plugin Flaw, Fake USPS Delivery Texts & Microsoft 365 MFA Tricks

Small business WordPress websites and Microsoft 365 accounts were the primary targets last week. We stripped out 41 enterprise software advisories to bring you the 4 threats that actually impact a 1–5 person company.

#1 Priority 5-Minute Team Action (Week #44)

Enable auto-updates on all WordPress plugins and audit who has admin access to your company website.

BleepingComputer Company Website
Critical • ⏱ 3 mins

Popular WordPress Contact & Booking Plugins Patched Against Site Takeover

Hackers are scanning small business WordPress websites for an unpatched flaw in popular appointment booking and contact form plugins. If your site runs an outdated version, attackers can create a hidden administrator account and redirect your customers to scam pages. Turning on automatic plugin updates in your WordPress dashboard prevents hackers from exploiting old plugins.

Krebs on Security Mobile & SMS
Medium • ⏱ 1 min

Holiday Shipping Smishing Texts Target Small Retailers & Solo Founders

A massive wave of fake USPS, UPS, and FedEx text messages claims a business supply shipment is held up due to an incomplete street address. The link leads to a credit card harvesting form that charges a tiny $0.30 'redelivery fee' before draining the business debit card days later. Shipping carriers never ask for a credit card number via a text message link to fix a delivery address.

iPhone / Android SMS, Company Debit Cards Source Advisory ↗
CISA Account Security
High • ⏱ 5 mins

CISA Warns of 'Push Bombing' Attacks on Microsoft 365 & Google Accounts

CISA issued guidance warning that criminals with stolen passwords are spamming employees with dozens of 'Approve Sign-In' phone notifications late at night until someone taps 'Approve' to make it stop. Small teams using simple one-tap push notifications are especially vulnerable when an owner is busy or distracted. Switching to number-matching or a hardware passkey stops this trick cold.

Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Authenticator Apps Source Advisory ↗
BleepingComputer Browser Extensions
Medium • ⏱ 3 mins

Malicious PDF Converter & Ad-Blocker Chrome Extensions Removed From Store

Security researchers uncovered several free Chrome extensions claiming to convert PDFs or find coupon codes that were secretly reading users' active browser tabs. Over 200,000 people installed these extensions, giving scammers access to webmail and store dashboards. Small teams should keep browser extensions to an absolute minimum.
